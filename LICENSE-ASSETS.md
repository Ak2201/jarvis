# Jarvis — name, character and artwork

The [MIT License](LICENSE) covers the **source code** of Jarvis. Jarvis is a fork of
[Coucou](https://github.com/louis-cfm/coucou) by Louis Raillé.

Coucou's name, its Mochi character, icons, sounds and media are **not** MIT-licensed and are
not part of this repository: Jarvis replaced them with its own name (**Jarvis**), its own
character name (**Juno**), and generated placeholder icons and sounds.

Juno has its own look: a domed visor bot with a dark glass visor, glowing cyan LED eyes
and an antenna whose light shows its state. It replaced Coucou's Mochi design on every
platform. The animation code (moods, emotes, outfits, physics) is still Coucou's MIT code.
